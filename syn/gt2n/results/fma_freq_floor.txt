titan_x7_fp32_fma_pipe frequency floor, GT2N elvt/w31/tt.
ABC delay-target sweep with buffering; RTL unchanged between rows.

  target      delay        GHz      area(um2)
  -D 200    401.81 ps     2.49       476.85
  -D 150    401.81 ps     2.49       496.81
  -D 120    401.81 ps     2.49       496.81

Identical delay, more area. 401.81 ps is a STRUCTURAL floor for this RTL,
not a tool setting. 3 GHz = 333.33 ps, so the remaining 17% must come from
re-partitioning the 8 pipeline stages into ~10-11. Critical path is 40 gate
levels with 6 buffers on it and no single dominant structure.

REJECTED: applying the tensor PE's sticky-mask rewrite to the FMA's E4
sticky. 401.81 -> 460.39 ps (worse). At 24 bits the per-bit comparisons run
in parallel into a balanced OR; the mask form inserts a barrel shift in
series ahead of it. The rewrite is a win at 137 bits and a loss at 24.
Reverted, with a warning comment in the RTL.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
THREE ATTEMPTS TO BREAK THE 401.81 ps FLOOR. ALL THREE MADE IT WORSE.

  1. tensor PE: split D2 across a pipeline register    434.52 vs 433.06 ps
                (and +7.8% area)
  2. FMA: sticky-mask rewrite on E4                    460.39 vs 401.81 ps
  3. FMA: split E7 into a 9th stage, registering the
     106-bit normalisation shift before the 81-bit
     sticky OR reduction                               476.91 vs 401.81 ps
                (and +7.8% area: 514.09 vs 476.85 um2)

Plus: stubbing logic OUT made it slower twice --
     E7 normalisation shift replaced by a constant     453.50 ps
     E4 alignment shift replaced by a constant         427.49 ps

That last pair is the tell. REMOVING a barrel shifter should never lengthen
the critical path. It does here because ABC's structural mapping is sensitive
enough to the surrounding cone that unrelated changes swing the result by
10-15%. Stub-based localisation of the critical stage is therefore not
reliable on this flow, and neither is chasing the last 17% by adding stages.

CONCLUSION: 401.81 ps / 2.49 GHz is a robust floor for this RTL on this PDK
with this toolchain. The design is load- and fanout-limited, not
depth-limited -- which is exactly why adding pipeline registers hurts: the
extra flops add load without shortening a path that was never too deep.

3 GHz is NOT reached and is not reachable by these means. What would plausibly
get there, none of it attempted here:
  - real place-and-route (ORFS), where a physical tool does buffering and
    sizing against actual wire loads rather than ABC's wireload="none" model
  - a richer standard-cell library; GT2N tops out at x4 drive for most cells
    and has no complex gates beyond aoi/oai33
  - a different FMA microarchitecture, not a re-partition of this one
