
TITAN X7 die budget on GT2N 2 nm -- 20,000 lanes
==================================================================
all per-block areas MEASURED by Yosys on GT2N cells (elvt/w31/tt)

  block                                   mm^2     share
  ------------------------------------------------------
  FP32 FMA lanes                          9.54     14.6%
  tensor PEs                              5.67      8.7%
  register files (FLOPS, as built)       50.20     76.8%
  ------------------------------------------------------
  TOTAL cell area                        65.40
  / 70% utilisation -> die               93.43   <- realistic

  vs reference dies:
    reticle limit         858 mm^2      10.9% of it   FITS
    H100                  814 mm^2      11.5% of it   FITS
    RTX 4090 (AD102)      609 mm^2      15.3% of it   FITS

  The register file is 77% of the area and holds 19.5 MiB.
  It is flip-flops because GT2N has NO SRAM and no memory compiler.

  ASSUMPTION (not measured): a compiled SRAM bitcell is ~10x
  denser than a flop for storage. On that assumption:
    register files ->     5.02 mm^2
    TOTAL cell area ->    20.22 mm^2
    die at 70% util ->    28.89 mm^2   (4.7% of a 4090 die)
